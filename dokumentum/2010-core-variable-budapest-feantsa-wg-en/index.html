Additional comments:

These tables contain the data of ETHOS 1.1. (Public space),  ETHOS 1.2. (Night shelter), ETHOS 3.1. (Homeless hostel) and ETHOS 4.1. (Other homeless hostel)

We have the same data since 1999, which we collect annually in February. The same data is available from 16 other citites and towns in Hungary.

Datacollection is organized annually by the  ‘3rd of February Working Group’,  a voluntary, non-official group, led by Péter Győri. Service providers participate on a voluntary basis, the questionnaires are either filled out by the homeless people themselves, or with the help of support staff, who ask the questions and write down the responses one-by-one. Analysis of the data is done and published by the 3rd of February Working Group.

We do not ask Questions 3.a. and 3.b., because they are not relevant in the Hungarian context. There are only very, very few people, who were born abroad or who do not have a Hungarian citizenship.

We ask Question 4 in a completely different way, because it is very rare for people to live outdoors with children, and it is impossible for someone to live in a homeless service with children.

Question 5.a we asked in certain years, but not in 2009.

Question 6. and 7. refer to the beginning of first episode of homelessness.

If we could reach some kind of a consensus regarding the core variables, and it would match the requirements of the the  ‘3rd of February Working Group’, the working group might decide to change its questions somewhat, or supplements them, or modify the ranges, groupings used in the analysis. 

I need to mention that in Hungary the Social Act and other pieces of legislation declare that all homeless services have to register the following data of their users :

Sex (2.)

Age (1.)

Citizenship (3.a)        

Place of birth (3.b)

Family status (... ‘4.’...)

Place of registering data (...’6’...)

But noone collects these data registered on an obligatory mandate on the bases of towns.
