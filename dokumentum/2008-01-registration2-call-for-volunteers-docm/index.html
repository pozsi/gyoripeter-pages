How many people sleep rough?

A call for the recruitment of volunteers for the headcount

The „February 3rd Working Group” (Győri Péter, Gurály Zoltán - Menhely Alapítvány, Gróf Andrtás – Twist Olivér Alapítvány, Fehér Boróka, Pelle József – BMSZKI) has carried out sociological research amongst homeless people living in shelters and hostels every year on the 3rd of February, since 1999. Every three years around the same date (first in 2005) we are also trying to count how many people sleep rough – we are recruiting volunteers for this task! 

During the night of 2008. February 2nd (going on 3rd) we will attemps once again to count how many people sleep (live) rough on a one given night in Budapest and in some other cities in Hungary – on the streets, in open, visible and invisible public places. We are calling for volunteers to help us achieve our goal!

How can you participate in the 2008 rough sleeper „census”?

1) If you see a homeless person who lives, sleeps in your immeadiate surroundings (in a public place, the staircase of a building, in something that is clearly not a flat), please call  06-20-572-9315 between 2008. Jan 18th and Feb 2nd, with the following information: exact location, gender, estimated age and state of the rough sleeper. 

If you have the possibility, you can also send this information to februar.harom@yahoo.com .

2) If you are ready to explore a given sector of the city either walking or by bike, and help us explore where rough sleepers live,

please come to the orientation meeting on

2008. February 2nd (Saturday) 4 PM 

at Dózsa György út 152, the Central Hall of BMSZKI,

where you will be given the „Rough sleepers registration sheet 2008.” 

and the chosen map section of the city.

The registration is trying to count those, who sleep in a public place, in the staircase of a building, in a non-flat setting on someone else's property on the night of 2008. február 2nd.

The only goal of the registration is to estimate the number of people living in such situations and to organise social services to suit their needs more efficiently.

A further goal of the 2008. registration is to achieve the following: we want the 2011 official Hungarian and EU census to cover rough sleepers to the full extent, as they are also citizens of our communities!

Registration is exclusively carried out by observation!

If you feel that the rough sleeper in question is in danger, call the 24 hour crisis hotline (338-4186) which will arrange to send some form of help.
